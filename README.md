ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship - Beginner Level Task
Project Overview
This project fulfills the beginner-level requirements for the ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship. The objective of this task is to analyze a small business dataset, extract key summary metrics, identify trends, and provide business-oriented observations.
I chose to analyze a Creative Services Sales Dataset, which tracks completed design and animation projects over a three-month period (August - October 2026).
Data Cleaning & Preparation
Before analysis, the raw data was processed to ensure accuracy:
Standardization: Ensured all dates were in a standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
Categorization: Extracted the Month from the date column to facilitate month-over-month trend analysis.
Validation: Checked for and removed any missing values or negative entries in the Revenue_USD and Turnaround_Days columns.
Key Summary Metrics
Total Revenue: $81,700
Total Projects Completed: 15
Average Deal Size: $5,446.67
Average Turnaround Time: 16.5 Days
Trend Analysis
1. Category-Wise Performance
Brand Redesign is the most lucrative category, generating the highest revenue per project (Average: $8,500) but requires the most operational bandwidth (Average Turnaround: 31 days).
Motion Graphics serves as the most stable revenue stream, with 6 projects completed at an average revenue of $5,716 and a reliable 15-day turnaround.
Web Animation yields the lowest revenue per project ($3,650) but has the fastest turnaround time (10.5 days), allowing for higher volume.
2. Monthly Revenue Trends
August: $25,400 (5 Projects)
September: $28,800 (5 Projects)
October: $27,500 (5 Projects)
Insight: Project volume is perfectly stable at 5 projects per month, but September saw a revenue peak due to closing a high-ticket Brand Redesign project with an Agency client.
3. Client Mix (Agency vs. Direct)
Agency Clients accounted for 8 projects (53.3% of volume) and generated $43,100 (52.7% of revenue).
Direct Clients accounted for 7 projects and generated $38,600.
Insight: Agency clients tend to request faster-turnaround items like Motion Graphics and Web Animations more frequently, providing steady cash flow.
Dashboard Layout Structure (Spreadsheet Implementation)
To visualize this data in Excel/Google Sheets, the dashboard is structured into three main sections:
Executive Summary (Top): Scorecards displaying Total Revenue, Avg Deal Size, and Avg Turnaround Time.
Trend Visuals (Middle):
A Line Chart showing Revenue by Month.
A Bar Chart comparing Average Revenue vs. Average Turnaround time per Service Category.
Distribution (Bottom): A Pie Chart illustrating the revenue split between Agency and Direct clients.
Business Conclusions & Recommendations
Protect Bandwidth for High-Ticket Items: Brand Redesigns are highly profitable but take over a month to complete. The business should cap these at 1-2 per month to ensure production bottlenecks do not delay the faster, high-volume Web Animation projects.
Leverage Agency Relationships: Agencies provide a consistent pipeline for Motion Graphics and Web Animations. The business should consider offering retainer contracts to these agencies to guarantee baseline monthly revenue.
Optimize Turnaround for Growth: Since Web Animation takes only ~10 days, marketing efforts should target direct clients for this specific service to easily increase monthly project volume without drastically straining resources.
